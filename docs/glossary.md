# Glossary

**Accessibility**  
The amount of useful opportunity reachable from a location after travel time, cost, capacity, transfers, and restrictions are considered.

**Actor**  
A decision-making participant such as a household, developer, industry, municipality, or private transport operator. An actor does not necessarily correspond to one rendered object.

**Bottleneck**  
A network element whose limited capacity or reliability materially constrains flows or development.

**Development**  
Construction, expansion, change of use, decline, or redevelopment performed by the simulation.

**Generalized cost**  
A common route-choice value combining time, monetary cost, delay, reliability, transfers, and policy penalties.

**Infrastructure**  
Player-shaped networks and facilities that provide access or capacity, including roads, railways, crossings, terminals, and later utilities.

**Local road**  
A low-capacity street primarily providing parcel access. It may be built autonomously within player-defined constraints.

**Private operator**  
A simulated organization that owns vehicles or services and chooses how to use available infrastructure.

**Regional demand**  
The bounded amount of population, employment, goods, and trade seeking locations in the playable region.

**Representative vehicle**  
A rendered vehicle sampled from an aggregate flow. It communicates activity without requiring every trip or shipment to be a persistent agent.

**Settlement**  
An emergent cluster of population and activity sharing a recognizable center and access pattern.

**Snapshot**  
A compact view of authoritative simulation state sent to rendering or UI systems.

**Strategic infrastructure**  
High-impact corridors and facilities reserved for the player, such as arterials, major crossings, highways, railways, and terminals.
