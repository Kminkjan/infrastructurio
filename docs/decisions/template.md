## Context

What concrete problem requires a decision? Include relevant product goals and constraints.

## Decision

State the proposed or accepted choice and its boundaries. Identify what remains open.

## Alternatives considered

Describe credible alternatives and why the chosen approach fits the evidence and goals.
Distinguish observed behavior from inference or assumptions.

## Consequences

Explain benefits, costs, risks, compatibility implications and follow-up work.

## Validation and acceptance boundaries

What evidence supports this choice? What still needs testing? Link delivery gates
separately; do not claim participant, performance or production validation without evidence.

## Revisit when

Name specific findings or requirements that would justify changing the decision.
